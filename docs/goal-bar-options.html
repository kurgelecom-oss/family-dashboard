Where the thinking is weak

  • You already have three goal systems. The board has Notion goals (weekly, monthly and long-term, through /api/mission), the hand-edited 1 Oct snapshot ($5k, $10k, $3k a week), and the corner Goals Intermission. A fourth place for goals would mean four sources of truth that drift apart within a month. The bar has to become the one place for 1/3/6-month goals, and the snapshot gets retired into it.
  • "3 months" from when? Rolling horizons never fall due, so nothing ever gets reviewed. Each goal needs a fixed due date (30 Nov, 31 Jan, 30 Apr), and passing that date must force a decision: done, missed, or the next goal. That is the "update as we move through time" mechanism. Without it the bar shows November's goal in March.
  • A goal without a number is a slogan. After two weeks the family stops reading slogans. Each goal should have a figure or a clear yes/no finish.
  • Dollar figures on the TV. Guests and Ansar's friends can see them. The Goals Intermission was deliberately built with zero currency. Decide whether money appears on the bar.
  • Constant motion on a screen that's always on becomes either wallpaper or an annoyance. Motion has to be slow, carry meaning, use only transform and opacity (so the TV browser keeps up), and stop under reduced motion. All three options follow that.
  • Height. A 30px bar pushes the 1920×1080 grid down. It reuses the retired strip slot (--strip-h), which every page already subtracts, so no layout math changes.
  • Who owns it. If nobody owns the review at month end, it won't happen. Monday presentation night (7pm) is the natural place to do it.

Straight assessment

  • Five of seven are “High”. The tiers don’t separate anything below goal 1, so the bar ranks by the order of your sheet (badges 1–7) and only Critical gets the clay badge.
  • Two goals have no finish line. “OmegaXL sustain/scale” and “repeatable proof of concept” show “—” until they get a number. Suggested: OmegaXL = net profit ≥ $X a week (or CPA ≤ $Y); proof = one product profitable 4 weeks running, done twice.
  • Goal 5 is two goals. $10k is the gate; the mentorship is what it unlocks. The bar treats it as $5k → $10k → mentorship starts. When it starts, the ~A$1,530/month subscription goes into PocketSmith and the household budget that day.
  • Dates conflict. The Money board says $5k by 27 Nov and $10k by 27 Dec; your sheet says 30 Nov and 31 Jan. The sheet is newer, so the Money board moves to match.
  • Weekly goals are hit-or-miss, not a countdown. That’s why the bar has two zones and why Option C no longer fits.
  • Everything is business or money on a family screen. So the bar shows percentages and days only, never balances. Worth one family or deen goal in the empty slots 8–10, so the bar isn’t only work.
