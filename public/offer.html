You enter the quote. Paste the supplier's Total column - one landed, delivered total per pack size - and everything else on this page is solved from it: four single-unit prices, a price for every pack, Shopify's cut at each of them, GST, the expected cost of returns, the ad budget each price can fund, and the most you can pay for a customer.
Totals are never linear, so nothing is multiplied out. Two units cost less than twice one because the supplier already prices freight at volume. That volume discount is margin you receive before you discount anything, and it is why each extra unit is nearly all contribution.
The discount is an output. Each pack is priced to leave the same share of the order before advertising, so what the offer "reads" as - 31% off, 42% off - is what the maths produced, not a number anyone guessed. Max cut is how much further you could go before a pack earns less than selling one.
Two constraints, and the higher one binds. A margin tier gives the minimum viable price. Needs for CPA gives the price a pack must reach to fund the traffic you are actually buying. On cheap goods the second is almost always the one that matters, and the page says so rather than letting a healthy-looking margin hide it.
Take rate is the only guess left. CPA is charged per order and every order is one pack, so the blended target CPA is the single number to hold ads to. At an all-singles mix it equals the single exactly and the ladder changes nothing.
Bundles improve profit and worsen cash at the same time. Four units per order is four times the stock, paid for weeks before the revenue lands. The scale panel and the stock-cash figure both run on blended cost per order so the two move together where you can see them. Shopify Payments Australia online card rates verified 4 Oct 2026 (Basic 1.75% + 30c, Grow 1.6% + 30c, Advanced 1.4% + 30c; Amex and international rates and the third-party gateway surcharge per the same published schedule). Tier cut-offs and the seeded CPA bands come from 2026 DTC and Meta-ads benchmark data: contribution-margin deciles (Level CFO, Commerce Catalyst), Meta purchase CPA by vertical (Triple Whale, AdLibrary), return rates and the cost of a return (ShipNetwork, 3PL Guys), and landed-cost composition (Syncost, Passport). CPA bands are US-sourced. The rate of CPA climb as spend rises has no published constant and is an operator dial, not a prediction.
